Fairfield County Town Guides

Watch local Discover CT episodes, explore town lifestyle, and connect with Mark Pires for buyer, seller, relocation, or home valuation guidance.

Fairfield

Coastal energy, classic neighborhoods, beaches, universities, restaurants, and one of Fairfield County's most balanced lifestyles.

New Canaan

Architectural heritage, beautiful neighborhoods, a polished downtown, and one of Fairfield County's most prestigious residential markets.

Westport

Beach town meets creative energy — Compo Beach, downtown shopping, arts, dining, and one of Fairfield County's most recognized lifestyles.

Greenwich

Luxury, waterfront, privacy, downtown energy, estates, train access, and one of the most recognizable real estate markets in the country.

Wilton

A scenic Fairfield County town known for space, privacy, beautiful roads, trails, and a quieter residential feel.

Darien

A premier coastal community with train access, beaches, beautiful neighborhoods, and a polished small-town feel.

Norwalk

A diverse coastal city with waterfront neighborhoods, restaurants, commuter access, beaches, and one of the widest lifestyle ranges in Fairfield County.

Stamford

City energy, corporate access, waterfront neighborhoods, restaurants, high-rises, historic districts, and a fast-moving real estate market.

Ridgefield

Historic charm, a vibrant Main Street, culture, restaurants, and a beautiful northern Fairfield County setting.

Easton

Rural beauty, open space, farms, reservoirs, and a quiet residential lifestyle minutes from Fairfield and Westport.

Weston

Quiet, wooded, private, and refined — Weston is for people who want space, nature, and a peaceful Fairfield County lifestyle.
