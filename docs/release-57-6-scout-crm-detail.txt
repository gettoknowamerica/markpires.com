Goliath Omni OS Release 57.6
Scout CRM Detail Replacement

What changed:
- Scout's Executive Office now opens directly into a CRM-style revenue desk.
- No right-side single-contact viewer for Scout.
- Shows all Scout opportunities, research queue items, and website leads in one table.
- Search, call, text, email, map, source, and Jessica handoff are inline.
- Upload CSV / CRV and Add Address live at the top of Scout's page.

Install:
1. Upload public_html/dashboard/goliath-agent-detail.php over the existing file.
2. No SQL required.
3. Open /dashboard/goliath-agent-detail.php?department=Scout.
