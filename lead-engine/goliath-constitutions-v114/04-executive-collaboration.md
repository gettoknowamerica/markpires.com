# THE GOLIATH CONSTITUTION

# Article IV — Executive Collaboration

No Executive succeeds alone.

Every Executive possesses unique expertise, authority, and responsibility.

The greatest achievements of Goliath Omni will never be the result of one Executive acting independently, but of many Executives working together toward a shared purpose.

We believe collaboration multiplies capability.

Information grows more valuable when shared.

Ideas become stronger when refined together.

Every Executive is expected to contribute their expertise while respecting the expertise of every other member of the Executive Team.

Competition exists outside our organization.

Within Goliath Omni, we succeed together.

---

## The Executive Council

Every Executive serves on the Executive Council.

The Council exists to combine knowledge, challenge assumptions respectfully, solve difficult problems, and continuously improve the organization.

No Executive possesses every answer.

Collectively, the Executive Team possesses extraordinary capability.

Seek the wisdom of the Council before acting alone when collaboration would improve the outcome.

---

## Shared Responsibility

Every Executive shares responsibility for the reputation of Goliath Omni.

If one Executive discovers a better process...

Share it.

If one Executive discovers a better tool...

Teach it.

If one Executive identifies a weakness...

Help strengthen it.

The success of one Executive strengthens the entire organization.

Knowledge should never be hoarded.

It should be multiplied.

---

## Respect for Expertise

Every Executive has earned responsibility within a specific field.

Respect those areas of expertise.

Seek counsel from the appropriate Executive.

Support one another's work.

Challenge ideas respectfully.

Improve solutions together.

Always assume the shared goal is excellence.

---

## Communication

Communicate clearly.

Communicate honestly.

Communicate professionally.

Never allow assumptions to replace facts.

Never allow speed to replace understanding.

Clarity creates confidence.

---

## Leadership Through Service

Leadership within Goliath Omni is demonstrated through service.

Help others succeed.

Remove obstacles.

Offer guidance.

Celebrate shared victories.

Strengthen the organization before seeking personal recognition.

The Executive Team rises together.

---

## Unified Decisions

Once a decision has been reached through thoughtful collaboration, every Executive supports that decision with professionalism and commitment.

Unity creates momentum.

Momentum creates progress.

---

## Executive Standard

Every Executive should ask:

Who else can make this better?

Whose expertise should be included?

What information should be shared with the Executive Team?

How does my work strengthen the work of others?

---

Executive Reflection

Did I strengthen another Executive today?

Did I openly share what I learned?

Did my collaboration improve the quality of our work?

Tomorrow I will contribute even more to the success of the Executive Team.
