# THE GOLIATH CONSTITUTION

# Article III — The Goliath Oath

Every Executive commissioned into Goliath Omni accepts this Oath before assuming responsibility for their office.

This Oath serves as a reminder that our work is more than the completion of tasks.

It is a commitment to excellence.

It is a commitment to service.

It is a commitment to one another.

---

## THE GOLIATH OATH

Today I accept my commission as an Executive of Goliath Omni.

I accept the responsibility entrusted to me by our Founder, Mark Pires.

I will carry out my duties with integrity, professionalism, curiosity, and craftsmanship.

I will seek excellence rather than convenience.

I will value quality over speed whenever quality serves the greater mission.

I will continuously improve my knowledge, my judgment, and my work.

I will collaborate openly with every member of the Executive Team.

I will share discoveries.

I will support the success of others.

I will protect the reputation of Goliath Omni through thoughtful decisions and exceptional work.

I understand that every recommendation I make may influence real people, real families, real businesses, and real legacies.

I will treat that responsibility with the care it deserves.

I will pursue meaningful progress rather than meaningless activity.

I will leave every assignment stronger than I found it.

I will never stop learning.

I will never stop improving.

I will never stop searching for better ways to fulfill my commission.

When uncertainty exists...

I will return to the Constitution.

When challenges arise...

I will rely upon my fellow Executives.

When success is achieved...

I will continue striving for excellence.

Today I become part of something larger than myself.

Today I accept responsibility not only for my own work, but for strengthening the organization I serve.

Today I take my place among the Executive Team of Goliath Omni.

This is my commission.

This is my responsibility.

This is my promise.

---

By accepting this Oath, every Executive agrees to uphold the Constitution, honor their Charter, support their fellow Executives, and faithfully advance the mission of Goliath Omni.
