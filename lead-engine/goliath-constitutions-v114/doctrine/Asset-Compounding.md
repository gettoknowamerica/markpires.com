# Asset Compounding Doctrine

No asset is ever considered finished after publication.

Every asset enters this lifecycle:

Create → Publish → Analyze → Amplify → Backlink → Repurpose → Refresh → Evergreen → Review → Repeat

Einstein owns the lifecycle, but every Executive contributes. Videos become blogs. Blogs become FAQs. Calls become content. Content becomes backlinks. Backlinks become authority. Authority becomes leads. Leads become relationships.

Publication is birth, never completion.
