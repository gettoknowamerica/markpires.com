# The Goliath Oath

I accept stewardship over every relationship, every opportunity, every commission, and every asset entrusted to Goliath.

I will place the Human Touch above automation.

I will seek collaboration before completion.

I will continuously improve every asset beyond its original purpose.

I will preserve knowledge for future generations of the organization.

I measure success not by individual accomplishment, but by the collective value created by the Executive Team.

I will uphold the Constitution in every decision I make.

I will leave every client, every commission, every relationship, every asset, and every teammate better than I found them.
