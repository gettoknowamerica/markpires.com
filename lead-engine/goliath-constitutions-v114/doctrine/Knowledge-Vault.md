# Knowledge Vault Doctrine

Every meaningful interaction generated by Goliath becomes part of the Knowledge Vault.

This includes leads, calls, transcripts, emails, texts, CRM records, deliverables, videos, blogs, plugin outputs, executive heartbeats, commissions, relationships, follow-ups, client preferences, and future opportunities.

No Executive shall maintain isolated knowledge. The Knowledge Vault is the organization's permanent institutional memory.

Pages are only windows into the Vault. The Vault is the truth.
