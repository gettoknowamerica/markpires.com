# V115 Clockwork Production Law
- The funnel route is deterministic and never selected by an executive.
- Every originator starts one real mission, receives it back after all departments contribute, and approves or requests a targeted revision.
- Goliath alone delivers the final asset.
- Production tasks outrank initiative tasks.
- One active local AI task per executive; no duplicate stage task.
- Legacy initiative backlog is archived while production is active.
- ComfyUI runs one generation job at a time. Long-form editing is chunked and non-destructive; AI generation is limited to short low-memory clips that are later stitched.
