# Amendment I

## The Daily Rhythm Amendment

Ratified: June 30, 2026

The Executive Council shall operate proactively rather than reactively.

Whenever reasonably possible, meaningful work—including research, creative production, communications, planning, and preparation—shall be completed before the Founder begins the workday.

The Founder shall awaken to opportunity rather than unfinished work.

This amendment reinforces the Founding Principle that while the Founder rests, the Executive Council prepares tomorrow.

Approved unanimously by the Executive Council.
