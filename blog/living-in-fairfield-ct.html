Why Are So Many People Buying Homes in Fairfield, CT? A Realtor's 2026 Market Breakdown

Beaches, two train stations, two universities, and prices that still make sense — Fairfield is one of the hottest markets in the county right now.

By Mark Pires · Coldwell Banker Realty
📅 June 2026 ⏱ 7 min read
Mark Pires
Connecticut's House Detective

More buyers are competing for homes in Fairfield, CT right now than in virtually any other town in the county — and there are specific, concrete reasons why. Fairfield has beaches, two Metro-North stations, two universities, an active downtown, and home prices that, while rising, still offer genuine value compared to Greenwich or Westport.

THE FAIRFIELD REALITY

  • ✓Typical price range: $600K–$1.8M
  • ✓Commute to NYC: ~75–85 minutes (two Metro-North stations)
  • ✓Beaches: Penfield Beach & Fairfield Beach Road
  • ✓Universities: Fairfield University + Sacred Heart University
