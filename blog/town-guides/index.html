Fairfield County Town Guides

Every Fairfield County town has its own pricing, pace, personality, school story, commute logic, and buyer profile. This hub brings together Mark Pires' town-by-town guides so buyers can compare communities more clearly and make better decisions.

Why Town-by-Town Context Matters

Greenwich is not Fairfield. Fairfield is not Stamford. Stamford is not New Canaan. Buyers who treat Fairfield County like one market usually make slower, more expensive decisions. These guides break down the real differences - schools, commute patterns, lifestyle, neighborhood feel, and pricing - from the perspective of someone who works these towns directly.
