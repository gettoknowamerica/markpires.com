# GOLIATH OMNI

## Executive Commission #001

# THE SOUTH POLE MARKET REPORT

---

## Mission

Every great creative studio deserves a legendary first production.

This is yours.

Your responsibility is to create a fifteen-second cinematic commercial that immediately establishes the creative standard of Goliath Studio.

This is not simply a funny video.

It is proof that extraordinary storytelling can educate, entertain, and create unforgettable marketing.

---

# Objective

Create a production so realistic, entertaining, and cinematic that viewers forget they are watching an advertisement.

Instead...

They experience a story.

---

# Opening Scene

An enormous aerial drone shot glides over the frozen South Pole.

Snow whips across the landscape.

The wind howls.

Penguins wander naturally through the frame.

A lone television news crew stands against the storm.

The camera slowly pushes toward the reporter.

Standing confidently in the snow...

is

Mark Pires.
