# V114 Orchestration Law

1. Every AI call begins by reading the organization constitution and the assigned executive charter.
2. Every mission owns one shared artifact. Executives improve that artifact instead of creating disconnected reports.
3. One stage is active per mission. Completion requires a tangible artifact that another person can immediately use.
4. When a stage passes its artifact gate, the production engine automatically starts the next stage.
5. Stale, failed, or abandoned stages are recovered automatically.
6. The originating executive reviews the enriched artifact before Goliath receives it.
7. Goliath coordinates, resolves blockers, publishes, distributes, and records delivery. Goliath does not inflate activity counts.
8. If no commissioned work exists, the morning boot creates a small number of high-value missions from real business priorities, never placeholder busywork.
9. Finished counts remain zero until Goliath delivers a real asset, lead package, publication, media file, campaign, or other usable outcome.
10. Supabase is not part of the V114 operating path. Hostinger MySQL is the system of record.
